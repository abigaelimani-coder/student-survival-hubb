Study methods that have actually worked

Explore different ways to improve your learning.

~Active Recall

Test yourself by trying to remember information without looking at your notes.

~Mind Mapping

Organize information using diagrams and connect ideas to make them easier to understand.

~Practice Questions

Always answer questions that test your understanding everytime after revising a topic. This will help you identify areas you need improvement You can use AI tools to do this. I highly recommend Gemini!as illustrated in the video

~Teach Someone

Explain a topic in your own words to somebody else to check whether you understand it.

~Past Paper Practice

Use past assessment papers to familiarise yourself with question formats, identify weak areas and practise applying what you have learned.
