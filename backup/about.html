THE INSPIRATION

Why I created this space

It started with a simple idea: everything in one place.

Being a student can be tough. Between assignments, revision, deadlines, personal goals and everyday responsibilities, there's already so much to keep up with.

Sometimes, finding the right resources makes things even more complicated. You have one app for planning, another website for studying, somewhere else for motivation and countless tabs open just to find what you need.

I wanted to change that.

I imagined one organised space where students could find useful resources, plan their work, explore study techniques, track their progress and find encouragement without having to jump between so many different platforms.

But I wanted it to be more than just a collection of links.

I want to include things I've personally explored, tested and found helpful throughout my own student journey. Resources and methods that have actually made a difference for me, so other students can discover them too.

Ultimately, I created Student Survival Hub because I understand that being a student isn't always easy. If something has helped me navigate that journey, perhaps it can help someone else along the way.
